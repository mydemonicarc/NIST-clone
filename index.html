About NIST

A Legacy of Innovation & Academic Distinction

Established in 1996, NIST University stands at the forefront of technical education, research, and holistic student development in India.

Brief Description of the College

NIST University (National Institute of Science and Technology) is an autonomous, multidisciplinary research and educational powerhouse situated across 65 sprawling acres at Institute Park, Berhampur, Odisha. Over the past three decades, NIST has created a benchmark in engineering, computing, technology, and management education.

Recognized by UGC and approved by AICTE, NIST has earned the prestigious NAAC 'A++' Grade. With over 65 world-class research and academic laboratories, a faculty pool of distinguished scholars from premier institutions, and extensive global tie-ups, the college empowers students to solve real-world problems through innovation, critical thinking, and social consciousness.

Autonomous Curriculum aligned with Industry 4.0
